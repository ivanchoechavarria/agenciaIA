In short: this site does not set advertising, analytics or tracking cookies and does not load third-party services. It only remembers in your browser, when you change the theme, whether you prefer light or dark mode.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website stores on your device. Similar technologies, such as the browser’s local storage (localStorage), serve a similar purpose. In this policy we refer to them collectively as “cookies”.

2. What we use on this site

The Site does not set its own HTTP cookies. It only uses this one local-storage entry, first-party (ERconnectIA):

erconnectia-theme

Type
Preferences (functionality you request)
Purpose
Remember whether you prefer the light or the dark theme.
When it is saved
Only when you press the theme switch button
Duration
Until you delete it
Consent
Not required: strictly necessary for a functionality you expressly request

3. Third-party services

  • Fonts. They are loaded from our own server: your browser does not connect to Google or any other third party to display them.
  • Hosting. Our hosting provider (Hostinger) may record technical connection data, such as your IP address, in server logs for security and operation.
  • WhatsApp and social networks. When you click a WhatsApp button or a social network link, you leave the Site and those services’ cookies and policies apply; we have no control over them.

4. How to manage your preferences

You can clear local storage or block cookies in your browser settings; the Site will keep working but will not remember your theme.

5. Legal framework

Colombia has no specific cookie law; when cookies allow a person to be identified, the principles of Law 1581 of 2012 apply (purpose, freedom and transparency, among others). For visitors in the European Union and the United Kingdom, we follow the ePrivacy Directive and the GDPR: cookies strictly necessary for a functionality requested by the user do not require consent; all others do.

6. Changes to this policy

If we introduce analytics (for example, a web analytics tool), personalization or marketing cookies in the future, we will update this policy and ask for your prior consent, with the option to accept or reject them separately.
