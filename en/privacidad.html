Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains how IAechavarría collects, uses, and protects the information of people who contact us, including through WhatsApp.

Information we collect

  • Name and WhatsApp phone number.
  • The content of the messages you send us, including voice notes (which are transcribed to text so we can process them).
  • Information you voluntarily share about your business or your needs (for example, which service you're interested in).

How we use this information

  • To answer your questions and follow up with you.
  • To schedule, confirm, or reschedule appointments when applicable.
  • To improve our automation services.

Part of this processing is assisted by artificial intelligence providers (such as Anthropic and OpenAI), who receive message content solely to generate a reply or transcription — it is not shared for model training purposes or with third parties unrelated to providing the service.

Who we share information with

We don't sell or rent your personal information. We only share it with the providers strictly necessary to operate the service (Meta/WhatsApp messaging infrastructure, database, and the AI providers mentioned above), under their own confidentiality terms.

How long we keep your information

We keep the conversation history for as long as there's an active or potential business relationship, and we delete it upon your request (see below).

Your rights

You can request to access, correct, or delete your information at any time by writing to us at contacto@iaechavarria.com.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, write to us at contacto@iaechavarria.com.
