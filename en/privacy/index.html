This policy explains how ERconnectIA collects, uses, stores and protects the personal data of people who visit this site or contact us, including through WhatsApp, in compliance with Colombian Law 1581 of 2012 and its implementing regulations.

1. Data controller

The data controller is {{legal.titular}}, Colombian Tax ID (NIT) {{legal.nit}}, domiciled at {{legal.domicilio}}, {{legal.pais}} (hereinafter “ERconnectIA”, “we” or “our”). Email: {{legal.email}} · WhatsApp {{legal.telefono}}. Requests and complaints about personal data are handled by the ERconnectIA team through the same email.

2. Legal framework

Article 15 of the Colombian Constitution, Law 1581 of 2012, Decree 1377 of 2013 (compiled in Decree 1074 of 2015) and any rules that amend or supplement them. For visitors in the European Union and the United Kingdom, the GDPR where applicable (see section 14).

3. What data we process

  • Identification and contact: name, WhatsApp phone number, email address and, if you share it, company and job title.
  • Content of communications: the messages you send us, including voice notes (transcribed to text so they can be processed), and any information you share about your business or needs.
  • Technical browsing data: IP address, browser and device type, and date and time of access, recorded on the server for security and operation.
  • Preferences: your light or dark theme preference, saved in your browser when you change it (see the Cookie Policy).

Sensitive data. We do not ask for sensitive data (such as health data, racial origin, sexual orientation or biometric data) through the Site or our business WhatsApp, and we ask you not to share it on those channels. If you choose to share it voluntarily, we will handle it with particular care and only to address your request; you are not obliged to provide it or to authorize its processing.

4. Purposes of processing

  • Answering your inquiries and information requests.
  • Preparing proposals, commercial follow-up and managing the commercial and contractual relationship.
  • Scheduling, confirming or changing appointments or meetings where applicable.
  • Improving and protecting our services and the Site, including security and abuse prevention.
  • Complying with legal and accounting obligations and responding to requests from authorities.
  • With your authorization, sending you commercial information about our services; you can stop receiving it at any time.

5. Authorization

By contacting us through WhatsApp, email or any other channel, and once informed of this policy, you give your prior, express and informed authorization to process your data for the purposes described. When a WhatsApp conversation starts, we will inform you of this policy and ask for your acceptance. You may revoke your authorization or request deletion of your data at any time, except where there is a legal or contractual duty to keep it.

6. Use of artificial intelligence

Part of the processing is carried out with the assistance of artificial intelligence providers (such as Anthropic and OpenAI), to whom the content of messages is sent only to generate a response or a transcription. That content is not shared for model-training purposes or with third parties unrelated to providing the service. We will let you know when a conversation is handled by an automated system.

7. Who we share information with

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We only share it with the providers strictly necessary to operate the service — Meta/WhatsApp messaging infrastructure, storage and databases, web hosting and the AI providers mentioned — who act as processors under their own confidentiality and security terms, and with authorities when required by law.

8. International transmission and transfer

Some of these providers are located outside Colombia, for example in the United States. We apply reasonable contractual and security measures, and by accepting this policy you authorize these transmissions and transfers, in accordance with Article 26 of Law 1581 of 2012 and the rules of the Colombian Superintendence of Industry and Commerce.

9. When we provide services to other businesses

When ERconnectIA provides its services to a clinic, aesthetics center or other business client, that business is the controller of its patients’ and customers’ data and ERconnectIA acts as a processor on its behalf, under the corresponding contract. This policy does not govern that processing, which is governed by the client’s own policy and that contract. If you are a patient or customer of one of those businesses, please exercise your rights with them.

10. Security and retention

We apply reasonable technical, organizational and administrative measures to protect data (such as encrypted HTTPS connections and access control). We keep data while there is an active or potential business relationship and for as long as needed to meet legal obligations and handle possible claims; afterwards we delete or anonymize it. The database remains in force while the purposes that justified its processing continue.

11. Data subject rights

Under Article 8 of Law 1581 of 2012, you have the right to:

  • know, update and correct your personal data;
  • request proof of the authorization you gave;
  • be informed, on request, of how your data has been used;
  • file complaints with the Superintendence of Industry and Commerce for infringements of the law;
  • revoke the authorization and/or request deletion of your data when legal principles, rights and guarantees are not respected;
  • access your personal data being processed free of charge.

12. How to exercise your rights

Write to {{legal.email}} stating your full name, a contact method and the right you wish to exercise, and include a description of the facts and any documents you want to rely on. We will respond as follows:

  • Inquiries: within a maximum of ten (10) business days, extendable by five (5) more business days, giving you the reasons.
  • Complaints (correction, update, deletion or revocation): within a maximum of fifteen (15) business days, extendable by eight (8) more business days. If the complaint is incomplete, we will ask you to complete it within five (5) days.

You may only go to the Superintendence of Industry and Commerce (Data Protection Delegation, www.sic.gov.co) after exhausting this process with us.

13. Minors

The Site and our services are not directed at minors and we do not knowingly collect their data. If we learn that we have received a minor’s data without the authorization of their legal representative, we will delete it.

14. Visitors in the European Union, the United Kingdom and other jurisdictions

If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, we process your data based on your consent, on pre-contractual steps or the performance of a contract, or on our legitimate interest in handling and managing inquiries. In addition to the rights above, you may request restriction of processing, data portability, object to processing, withdraw your consent at any time and lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority. Write to us at {{legal.email}}.
